Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Our Approach

AreaGrab is a local-first Windows application. It does not require an account and does not upload your screenshots. The free 1.1 version makes no network requests.

What AreaGrab Stores Locally

  • Settings in %APPDATA%\AreaGrab\settings.json (folder, hotkey, format, UI preferences).
  • Screenshots in the folder you choose, or not at all if you disable saving.
  • Logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AreaGrab\logs, used only for diagnostics. Logs do not contain screenshot pixels or window titles.

You can delete these files at any time. Uninstalling AreaGrab removes the application; settings and screenshots are left for you to delete.

Clipboard

When you take a screenshot, AreaGrab places the image in the Windows clipboard so you can paste it with Ctrl+V. The clipboard is shared with other applications and may be recorded by Windows clipboard history (Win+V) if you enabled it. Do not capture sensitive information unless you intend to paste it.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number is required to use the app.
  • No screenshots or clipboard content are transmitted to us.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies are used by the app.

Website

This website is a static page. It does not set cookies. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and reliability; those logs are not combined with app data.

Purchases and Third Parties

If you buy a paid license in the future, the payment is processed by a third-party marketplace or payment provider. That provider collects the information needed to complete the purchase and handle refunds under its own privacy policy. We receive only the information needed to issue and support your license.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always posted on this page.
